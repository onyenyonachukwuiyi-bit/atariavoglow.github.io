Company Profile

Climate-smart farming turned into premium value-added products.

ATARI FARMS Limited focuses on climate-smart agriculture, circular agribusiness, and avocado-based value creation. The company connects smallholder farming with premium consumer goods, giving Uganda-grown avocado a stronger route into beauty, hair care, and wellness markets.

Through Avoglow, Atari Farms turns avocado oil, avocado seeds, and related by-products into products such as Pure Avocado Oil, Avocado-Rosemary Oil, Avocado-Lavender Oil, Root & Revive Hair Elixir, Intensive Hair Butter, AvoChai, and other natural wellness concepts.

Mission

To build climate-smart avocado value chains that empower farmers, reduce waste, and create premium natural products for everyday wellness.

Vision

To become a leading African avocado innovation brand known for sustainability, farmer inclusion, and high-quality beauty and wellness products.

Inclusion

Market access, women and youth participation, and farmer empowerment are central to the Atari Farms growth model.

Innovation

Avocado by-products are reused creatively, including wellness products such as AvoChai made from avocado seeds.
